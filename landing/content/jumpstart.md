Jumpstart Signal is a free daily stock screener. Every trading day it runs ~5,300 US-listed common stocks through a 5-stage pipeline that combines fundamental analysis, technical indicators, AI-powered news sentiment, and ESG/SRI exclusions (no fossil fuels, tobacco, weapons, gambling, and more). A handful of **SPOTLIGHT**, **OPPORTUNITY**, and **MONITOR** signals come out the other end, split into Growth ($5-$20) and Momentum ($20-$100) buckets.

Scoring is 100% data-driven with no manual overrides. Signal weights are walk-forward validated across 14 years (2012-2025), and results land in your inbox each day as well as in interactive web reports with TradingView charts.
