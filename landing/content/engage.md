## The Flexpack: A Flexible Pack of Hours

A **Flexpack** is a prepaid service package of **8 days (64 hours)** of flexible consulting and contracting support.

- **Use it your way:** continuously or intermittently, across multiple concurrent projects and sessions.
- **Flexible increments:** anything from 30 minutes up to a full working day.
- **Valid for 90 days** from the date of payment. Unused hours do not roll over.
- **Only active time is drawn down:** hours are deducted for time spent on your work, not for time lost to dependencies, waiting on information, coordination delays, or other blockers.
- **Prepaid in EUR (€):** invoices are DUR (Due Upon Receipt), and hours are available once payment is received.

### Why it works:

The Flexpack is a more efficient and economical way to access specialist support. Time is **micro-sliced** and pointed at whatever delivers the most value right now, so the hours go into your work instead of into waiting around.

### What you can use it for:

- Consulting and concrete deliverables
- Marketing and technical marketing
- Platform and code work
- Design / Audit
- Technical Project / Product Management
- Product activities

Work is performed asynchronously and synchronously with individuals or whole client teams.
